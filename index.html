StarSums — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 • Package ID: com.loitongbam.starsums

Short version: StarSums works 100% offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from children or adults. There are no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases.

1. Information We Collect

None. StarSums does not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or any other personal or non-personal data.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Game progress — such as pilot nickname, stars earned, unlocked rocket skins, badges, and accuracy summaries — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time from the Grown-Up Corner inside the app or by uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

StarSums is designed to be safe for children and families. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or any age), display third-party advertising, or link out to social networks or external stores. Settings and progress resets are placed behind a Grown-Up Corner math check.

4. Third-Party Services

StarSums does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or cloud databases. All sound effects and artwork are bundled directly inside the application.

5. Changes to This Policy

If future updates ever change how StarSums handles data, this policy will be updated prior to releasing those changes.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at arun.loitongbam@gmail.com.
